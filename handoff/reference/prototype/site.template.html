BT Smör

A butter-inspired variable typeface in the spirit of GT Walsheim and Otto Baumberger’s poster lettering. Razor-sharp when cold. Gooey when warm. Drawn for Norwegian.

Scroll to turn up the heat

01 — The idea

Most typefaces stay the way you left them. BT Smör doesn’t. Turn up the heat and its corners soften, its drips run, its bubbles rise, and the whole line ends up in a puddle.

02 — MELT axis

Smør

Solid.

Sharp as a fresh-cut block. Flat-cut terminals, mitred corners, and feet cut dead level on A, V, W and X. This is the typeface before anyone turns on the stove.

Soft.

Corners start to give. Terminals round off and the first drips form under crossbars and curves.

Gooey.

Letters lean, droop and sink. Drips run, droplets fall, and bubbles begin to rise.

Puddle.

Everything ends in the pool. One continuous puddle runs under the whole line, and the foam sits on top.
